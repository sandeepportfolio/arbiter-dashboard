# Arbiter — Independent State Analysis, 2026-07-25

Written from live state, not from the handoff notes. Every claim below was derived from the running
system (API, Postgres, container logs, venue APIs) and, where material, independently re-verified by a
second agent instructed to refute it. Where this document contradicts the handoff notes or the task
brief, the contradiction is called out explicitly.

---

## 1. Headline

The platform is **operationally healthy and correctly gated, but it is not trading — and that is the
correct behaviour, not a fault.** There is genuinely no profitable edge available on the markets it is
permitted to trade. The real constraints are (a) a tradable universe that has collapsed to 2 usable
venue-pairs, and (b) fee drag that exceeds observed cross-venue mispricing.

Two things found during this audit are genuinely serious and were **not** in the notes:

1. **The ops API served every financial detail publicly with no authentication**, reachable through
   the live Cloudflare tunnel. (Now fixed — see §7.)
2. **The container is being OOM-killed repeatedly** (6 confirmed kills), and `docker inspect` reports
   `OOMKilled=false`, which is a false negative that hid it.

## 3. Why no trades have fired

**No order of any kind has been submitted since 2026-07-18** — six days.

This is not a broken pipeline. The auto-executor logged **zero skip reasons** because it is never
reached: the scanner publishes zero opportunities. Tracing that to ground:

- 43 mappings are `allow_auto_trade=true`.
- **Only 5 of them have two-sided live quotes.** The other 38 are Kalshi-only, so an arbitrage is
  mathematically undefined — 40 of the 43 are NFL/NCAA championship futures whose Polymarket legs
  never quote.
- Computing net edge on those 5 using **the system's own fee functions**:

| Canonical | Best route | Net edge |
|---|---|---|
| GOP_SENATE_2026 | YES@kalshi 0.57 + NO@forecastex 0.41 | **−0.25¢** |
| DEM_SENATE_2026 | YES@kalshi 0.45 + NO@forecastex 0.55 | −2.25¢ |
| MLS TIE ×3 | various | −2.21¢ … −4.19¢ |

Every route is negative. `best_edge_cents: 0.0` is the scanner flooring negatives — it is telling the
truth. With `MIN_EDGE_CENTS=7`, a trade needs total cost ≤ $0.908; the two venues sit within 1–3¢ of
each other while fees are ~2.2¢/contract. **The gate is correct and the market is efficient.**

### The phantom-edge trap (important)

Across the **86** canonicals that *do* have two-sided quotes, 19 routes show ≥7¢ net edge — up to
**+80¢**. These are all mis-mappings, not opportunities. Cross-venue implied-YES divergence on the top
four is **69.5¢–82.5¢** against an 8¢ coherence gate — the two venues are quoting different questions.
None are confirmed or auto-trade-enabled.

**The defense stack is working exactly as designed.** This is the same signature as the July-10
party-swap incident, and this time the system is refusing it. That is the single strongest piece of
evidence in this audit that the platform is safe to leave armed.

## 4. Profitability — the number that matters

Lifetime closed-arb realized PnL is **+$111.145** across 143 closed arbs. But split by *trade* date:

| Traded | Closed arbs | Realized | W/L |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-04 | 16 | **+$65.52** | 11/5 |
| 2026-05 | 94 | **+$68.41** | 36/5 |
| 2026-06 | 13 | **−$11.89** | 5/7 |
| 2026-07 | 20 | **−$10.90** | 1/3 |

**April–May (CLI era): +$133.93. June–July (auto-execution era): −$22.79.**

The June/July losses are not a bookkeeping artifact — they were *revealed* by the invisible-fill
correction, which restated trades that had been mis-booked as profitable. Of the 18 genuinely-new
July arbs, net was +$0.04 (breakeven).

**Structural finding:** `profitability/validator.py` evaluates a *lifetime* aggregate. It is dominated
by April–May and therefore **cannot detect regime decay**. It currently reports
`validated_profitable` while the live-trading era is net negative. It also counts unrealized booked
edge from open positions. This gate authorises live trading — it should be windowed.

- **One phantom position:** ARB-001362 shows an 11-lot Polymarket MLB position, unhedged 145h. The

  This is a stale internal record driving a false violation, plus an unterminating `recovery_check`

**Real defect found:** the stranded auto-close path placed 26 Kalshi orders ($21.43 + $1.27 fees) in an
uncapped re-fire loop — escalating 2→4→8 lots and repeating ~15× over 81 minutes, accumulating 138
shares against a single **8-share** stranded leg (~17× over-hedge), with **zero rows in
`execution_orders`**. The flag being on is intentional; the missing cumulative cap and audit trail are
the bug.

## 6. Health

- **6 OOM kills confirmed** in the Docker VM kernel log (`global_oom`, cgroup = arbiter-api-prod),
  each 3–4s before a boot. `docker inspect` says `OOMKilled=false` — a false negative, because Docker
  only sets that for *cgroup* OOM. **No memory limit is set** in a 3.81 GiB VM; the process dies at
  ~3.42 GiB and sits at 1.93 GiB after 2h. It leaks and dies every few hours, and a SIGKILL skips the
  graceful shutdown path — a plausible generator of naked legs.
- **429 storm on `/iserver/secdef/strikes`**, ~4/min for 24 min then self-resolved. Root cause: a
  NO-conid backfill loop re-probing unresolvable targets, **43 of which carry the literal junk conid
  `'0'`**. It reportedly occupies ~60% of the discovery cycle's wall clock, starving live quote
  snapshots.
- **IBKR OAuth (`ARBITERFX`) is still not activated** — expected. Not enabled, not touched.
- Balances fresh (<1s), collectors healthy, circuit closed, kill switch disarmed.

## 9. The honest bottom line on "trading profitably across all three venues"

- **All three venues are funded, connected, and quoting.** Kalshi↔ForecastEx and Kalshi↔Polymarket are
  both live and have both executed real trades historically.
- **`polymarket:forecastex` has never completed a single trade** — zero, lifetime. It is a venue pair
  that exists on paper only.
- **The system will not fire today**, because the best available net edge is −0.25¢ and the floor is
  +7¢. Arming it harder would not produce trades; it would only produce the phantom-edge trades the
  coherence gate is currently, correctly, refusing.
- The lever that would actually matter is **expanding the two-sided quote surface** (86 canonicals
  have two-sided quotes; only 5 are permitted to trade) — but every candidate must clear the coherence
  gate first, and that is exactly the discipline that prevented a repeat of the party-swap loss.
